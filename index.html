Before You Ask

Quick answers.

CLESTA is open to all incoming freshers of the batch, organized by Digiquest Academy. Valid student ID is mandatory at entry.
No outside food, drinks, or beverages are permitted inside the venue. Refreshments will be available on-site.
All ticket sales are final and non-refundable once purchased, though passes can be reissued for name corrections before the event.
